# Coach User Guide (Web & Mobile)

## Introduction
The Coach role coordinates training regimens, roster management, match tactics, and athlete performance evaluations.

## Key Workflows
1. **Roster Review**: View athletes assigned to your team and inspect positions and availability.
2. **Training Schedules**: Review upcoming training sessions and drill notes.
3. **Attendance Logging**: Mark attendance during or immediately following sessions.
4. **Match Day Tactics**: Check upcoming fixtures, opposing lineups, and log post-match summaries.
5. **Leave Requests**: Apply for personal leave for HR approval.
